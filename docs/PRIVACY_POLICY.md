# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

At ChatForge AI, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** Email address, name, company name
- **Training Data:** Text content, documents, and files you upload to train your chatbots
- **Bot Configuration:** Bot names, instructions, customization settings
- **Messages:** Conversations between your chatbot and end users

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, time spent on the platform
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, operating system
- **Cookies:** Session cookies, preference cookies (see Cookie Policy below)
- **Log Data:** Error logs, access logs, API usage

### 1.3 Third-Party Information
- **Authentication:** If you sign up via OAuth providers (future feature)
- **Payment Information:** Credit card information (processed by third-party payment processors in future)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 To Provide the Service
- Create and manage your account
- Process and store your training data
- Generate AI chatbot responses using your data
- Enable chatbot embedding on your websites
- Provide customer support

### 2.2 To Improve the Service
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
- Monitor and improve performance
- Develop new features and functionality
- Conduct research and analytics

### 2.3 To Communicate With You
- Send service-related notifications
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Send important updates about the Service
- Share product announcements (with your consent)

### 2.4 For Security and Legal Compliance
- Detect and prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect our rights and property

## 3. How We Share Your Information

### 3.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data
We will never sell your personal information or training data to third parties.

### 3.2 Service Providers
We share information with trusted third-party service providers:

- **OpenAI:** We use OpenAI's API to generate embeddings and chatbot responses. Your training content is sent to OpenAI for processing but is not used to train their models (per OpenAI's API data usage policy).
- **Supabase:** We use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage.
- **Hosting Providers:** We use cloud hosting services (Vercel) to deliver the Service.

### 3.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental request.

### 3.4 Business Transfers
If ChatForge AI is acquired or merged, your information may be transferred to the new entity.

### 3.5 With Your Consent
We may share information for purposes not listed here with your explicit consent.

## 4. Data Separation and Security

### 4.1 Multi-Tenant Security
- Each user's data is logically separated in our database
- Your training data is only accessible to your account
- Chatbot responses are generated using only your training data
- Other users cannot access your bots or conversations

### 4.2 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data
- Secure authentication (Supabase Auth)
- Regular security audits and updates
- Access controls and monitoring

### 4.3 AI and Embeddings
- Training content is converted to vector embeddings for semantic search
- Embeddings are mathematical representations and cannot be reverse-engineered to original text
- Your embeddings are stored securely and used only for your chatbots

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Active Accounts
- We retain your data as long as your account is active
- Training data and bot configurations are stored indefinitely while active

### 5.2 Account Deletion
- You can delete your account at any time through the dashboard
- Upon deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days
- Some information may be retained for legal compliance (e.g., transaction records)

### 5.3 Backup and Logs
- Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days
- Log data may be retained for up to 1 year for security purposes
